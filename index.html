About Me

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Anatomy and Neurobiology at Boston University, working in the Wallace Lab. My research investigates how topographically organized basal ganglia subcircuits represent threat/reward conflict. My work combines genetic neuroanatomical tract tracing, loss-of-function behavioral approaches, and optical interrogation of circuit activity. I am also an experienced educator in the biomedical sciences, with teaching experience in lecture and laboratory settings across a wide variety of student populations. I have experience as a skilled cadaveric dissector for medical research and education. I am actively searching for postdoctoral fellowship opportunities. Please feel free to get in touch.

Curriculum Vitae

Skills

Science Illustration

Illustration experience gained primarily from both graduate and undergraduate teaching (*but also from lifelong interest in visual art). Particular emphasis on cellular-level concepts related to introductory mammalian physiology courses, such as cellular organization of tissues in the renal and digestive systems. Extensive experience with illustration of the neuroanatomical pathways found in standard neuroanatomy courses, including afferent and efferent somatic and visceral pathways and complex brainstem circuit diagrams such as the visuo-oculomotor reflex. Portfolio available upon request.

  • Since 2018

Cadaveric Dissection and Human Anatomy Education

Participated either fully or partially in 5 human dissections at Colorado State University and Boston University. Responsible for human brain specimen management during appointments as a Functional Neuroanatomy lab instructor. Performed a variety of special dissections including a removal of the heart and lungs as a functional unit, isolation of the respiratory tree and associated pulmonary vasculature, craniotomies/brain removals, and special brain dissections to reveal the insular cortex.

  • Since 2021

Stereotaxic Surgery (Mouse)

Extensive experience with targeted intracranial injection via stereotaxic apparatus of adeno-associated viruses for genetic neuroanatomical tract tracing as well as toxins for regionally selective dopaminergic ablation. Extensive experience with optical fiber implantation for in vivo freely moving fiber photometric recording.

  • Since 2021

Histology and Image Analysis

Standard mouse brain histology with PFA perfusion, microtome use, immunofluorescence staining and slide scanner use. Experienced with QuPath, FIJI, and ABBA brain registration software. Designed custom workflows to perform 3-dimensional voxel-based statistical analyses on cell location data collected from registered slices.

  • Since 2021

Electronics Design

Used KiCAD software to create custom printed circuit boards capable of interfacing 1) LabJack data acquisition boards, 2) Arduino microcontrollers, 3) Fiber Photometric / Miniscope Data Acquisition, and 4) Video Cameras to automate task structure and data streaming in custom behavioral tasks.

  • Since 2021

Coding, Data Management, and Statistical Analyses

Designed low-level software to interface between Arduinos and a LabJack DAQ to automate behavioral task structure via MATLAB. Extensive experience designing data handling workflows for large behavioral datasets for flexible analysis in both MATLAB and Python. Developed Generalized Linear model pipelines for fiber photometric recordings and behavioral predictors.
